# Pixel office (Phase 2)

The Office tab is a pixel-art world. Agents walk to the room that matches what they are really
doing, wait under a "!" when they need you, and your desk opens an approvals + auto-approve panel.

## What drives where an agent stands

`Signals.kt` builds an `AgentSignal` per agent from live data, `OfficeSim.kt` acts it out:

| Real state | Where they go |
| --- | --- |
| pending approval or connector request (or status `waiting_approval`) | queue in front of your desk, "!" bubble |
| status `error` | their desk, red "!" |
| status `paused` / `sleeping` | break room couch, "Zzz" |
| an active build for them | workshop |
| used a connector in the last 20 s (or 60 s while `working`) | room for that connector (see `placeForConnector`) |
| status `thinking` | their desk, thought bubble |
| status `working` | their desk |
| idle | wander: coffee, couch, war room, library, chat with a coworker |

Agents stay in a room at least 4 s before changing (`MIN_HOLD`) so quick status flips do not make them run around.

Rooms and the features they stand for: Boss office (approvals, auto-approve), War room (Project),
Library (diary / what agents learned), Web room (each agent's browser), Mail (email and chat
connectors), Server room (deploy and data connectors), Workshop (builds), Finance (PayPal, Polar,
revenue), Break room (idle), Bullpen (12 desks, plus a notice board).

Tap a room to see what it is for and open that screen. Tap an agent for their card. Tap your desk
(or the "! n to approve" button) for the desk panel.

## Desk panel and auto-approve safety

- Low and medium risk requests can be approved or denied right in the panel.
- High risk requests and connector requests open the full Approvals screen first.
- Auto-approve switches are offered only for: build projects, install connectors, other low-risk.
- These stay manual here: connector actions (they include payments), send email, external actions,
  publish, delete, spend on APIs. If one is already on from elsewhere it is listed with a Turn off button.

## The art

`res/drawable-nodpi/office_pixel_base.png` (floors, walls, wall decor) and
`office_pixel_objects.png` (furniture atlas, drawn y-sorted with the agents so desks hide seated
legs). Both are generated by `tools/office_gen/`:

    cd tools/office_gen
    python3 gen_office.py && python3 export_kotlin.py

then copy the two PNGs to `res/drawable-nodpi/` and `OfficeMapData.kt` to `ui/office/world/`.
Needs Python 3 and Pillow. Change a room or move furniture in `gen_office.py`: collision, spots and
tap areas are regenerated together, so nothing has to be placed by hand.
`OfficeMapData.kt` is generated, do not edit it.

## Performance (Helio G36 / 4 GB)

- Base map is 704 x 1664 and the furniture atlas is 512 x 451: about 75 KB of PNG in total.
- Decoded without density scaling; redraw about 30 fps and only while the screen is visible.
- Furniture is culled when off screen; pathfinding is a breadth-first search over a 22 x 52 grid.

## Debug

Long-press the THE OFFICE title: blocked tiles (red), spots (orange), tap areas (yellow), and the
world x/y of each tap.
